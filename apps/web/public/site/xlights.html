We still love xLights.

pixl exists because of it, borrows its ideas, reads and writes its files, and does not try to replace it.

xLights is the free, open source program most pixel shows are sequenced in. It has been built over many years by volunteers, it has more effects than we will ever have, it runs on your own machine at native speed, and it has a community that answers questions at midnight in December. If you are new to this hobby, start there: xlights.org. Our docs assume you know it.

How the two fit together

Use desktop xLights whenUse pixl when
You have a big show and a machine you are happy sequencing atYou are away from that machine, or on a laptop with nothing installed
You want the full effect library and the fastest renderYou want autosave, snapshots and a project two people can open
You are doing anything with hardware xLights supports and we do notYou want a lyric timing or a new shader made for you by a service
Rendering time matters more than where you areWhere you are matters more than rendering time

Moving between them

  • Import your xLights layout (xlights_rgbeffects.xml) and sequences (.xsq).
  • Export .fseq for FPP and other players, exactly as xLights would.
  • Lyric timings download as .xtiming files for xLights' Import Timing Track.
  • Shaders download as .fs files for xLights' Shader effect, and they are checked against xLights' shader dialect before they reach the gallery.
Not everything comes across, and we would rather tell you than let you find out. The import reports what it could not map. Our effect library is a fraction of xLights', and the browser preview is slower.

Same licence, same spirit

xLights is GPL-3.0. So is pixl. That is not a coincidence: the hobby runs on people sharing what they built, and we want anything we make to be as free to take and improve as the thing that inspired it. pixl is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the xLights team.
